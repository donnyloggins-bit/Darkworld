Privacy Policy

DARKWORLD · Last updated October 2026

DARKWORLD is a location-based game. This page explains what it collects, why, and how to delete it. The short version: your exact location stays on your phone, nothing is sold, and there are no ads or trackers.

Your location

  • The game uses your GPS position on your device to place monsters, shrines and raids on the streets around you, and to count the distance you walk.
  • Your exact location is never sent to us.
  • If you are signed in, the game reports which roughly 1 km grid square you are in, about every two minutes, so other players can see a count of "hunters nearby." Other players only ever see a number, never you or your square. We keep only your most recent square, and it is deleted with your account.
  • Location is used only while the game is open. It is not tracked in the background.

Camera

The optional AR view shows your camera feed behind the monsters. The video stays on your device. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.

Accounts (optional)

You can play without an account. If you create one, we store:

  • Your email address and password (the password is stored hashed by our authentication provider) so you can sign in.
  • Your game save (character, items, progress) so it follows you between devices.
  • Your raid damage, recorded with your character name, to share out raid rewards.

Public to other players: your character name, class, level, distance walked and bestiary count on the leaderboard, and your character name and damage on a raid's damage board. Your email is never shown.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores accounts and game data.
  • Netlify hosts the web version.
  • CARTO serves the map tiles and Google Fonts serves the game's fonts. Like any website, they receive your IP address when your device downloads tiles or fonts.

We do not sell or share your data for advertising. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Account (☁ button) → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently erases your login, cloud save, presence and raid record.
  • On your device: uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) removes the local save.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data by email (below).

Children

DARKWORLD is a horror-themed game that is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
